How It Works

From enquiry to delivery

No surprises on the invoice. Every job is scoped, quoted, and approved before we touch the car.

01

Tell us about your car

Send your vehicle make, model, and what you want done via WhatsApp, call, or the quote form. Photos help us scope it faster.

02

Free inspection & quote

We inspect the vehicle (or review your photos), recommend parts and options, and send a clear, itemized quote — no obligation.

03

Approve & schedule

Once you approve, we confirm parts availability and lock in a drop-off date and realistic completion window.

04

Build & quality check

Our technicians complete the work, then run a fit-and-finish quality check — panel gaps, alignment, electronics, and road test.

05

Handover & support

You collect a fully explained vehicle, with care guidance for wraps, coatings, and any new systems installed.
