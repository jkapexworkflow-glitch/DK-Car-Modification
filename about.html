Who We Are

Craftsmanship first.
Everything else follows.

DK Car Modifications is a Kampala-based workshop built around one idea: a modification should look like it was designed into the car, not bolted onto it.

Our Approach

Built for real roads, finished like a showpiece

Uganda's roads — from smooth tarmac to murram and everything between — put real demands on a modified car. We don't just chase a look; we build setups that survive daily driving in Kampala while still delivering the finish and stance our clients want.

Every job, whether it's a single accessory fit or a full transformation, goes through the same process: inspect, quote, approve, build, quality-check. No surprises, no shortcuts on fit and finish.

Our working principles

Quote before we touch it

Every job is itemized and approved before work begins — no scope creep, no invoice surprises.

Fit-and-finish is non-negotiable

Panel gaps, alignment, and electronics are checked as standard, not as an upsell.

Built for how you actually drive

Stance and performance choices account for real Kampala road conditions, not just showroom photos.

Where We Are

Based in Mukono

Our workshop is based in Mukono, right on the Kampala–Jinja corridor, and we serve clients across Mukono and the greater Kampala metro area. Open all week — reach out to confirm drop-off logistics for your area.
